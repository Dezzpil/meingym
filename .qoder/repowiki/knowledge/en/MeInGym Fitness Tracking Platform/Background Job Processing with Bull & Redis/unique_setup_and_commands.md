Workers are started with `npm run workers`. Two jobs are auto-scheduled on worker startup: inactive period checks at midnight daily (`0 0 * * *`) and orphaned image cleanup at 3 AM daily (`0 3 * * *`). Jobs can also be enqueued programmatically via the exported helpers or through the `/api/jobs` endpoint. Redis connection is configured via `REDIS_HOST` and `REDIS_PORT` environment variables.
