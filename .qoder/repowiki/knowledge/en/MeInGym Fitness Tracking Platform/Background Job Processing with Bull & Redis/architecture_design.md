The module is organized around three layers: `config.ts` centralizes Redis connection settings, queue/job name constants, and default job options (3 attempts, exponential backoff, removeOnComplete). `queues.ts` instantiates three Bull queues (`scores`, `periods`, `images`) bound to the shared Redis config and registers each processor against its corresponding job name. `index.ts` is the entry point that side-effects import all processors (triggering their registration), exports helper functions (`scheduleScoreCalculation`, `scheduleCheckInactivePeriods`, `scheduleCleanupOrphanedImages`) for enqueueing jobs from other modules, and auto-schedules the two cron jobs on startup. Each processor in `processors/` follows a uniform pattern: accept a Bull job, use the Prisma singleton from `@/tools/db`, call core business logic from `@/core/*`, and always `prisma.$disconnect()` in a finally block. Dependency direction is strictly inward — processors depend on `@/tools/db` and `@/core/*`, never on the rest of the app.
